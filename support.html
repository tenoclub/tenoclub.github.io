Common questions

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and use Report or Block. Blocking is instant and silent — they are not told, and you disappear from each other.

Why can I not message someone yet?

In Date mode you match by liking each other. The first two hours are hers to say hi — after that you can both write. In Friend mode you can write first, without matching.

How do Clubs events work?

Ask to join, and the host approves or declines. Once the event has started the host marks who actually came — that is what fills your attendance badge.

What is a Buddy?

A Buddy says "I think we would get on really well". You get three a month, and sending one opens the chat straight away.

I bought something and nothing happened

Payments are not live yet — no card is ever charged in this version. If you were charged, write to us and we will sort it out.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Delete account. It removes your profile, photos, messages and events — it is not reversible.
