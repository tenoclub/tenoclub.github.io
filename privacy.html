Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Teno ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Teno mobile application. Teno is operated from Istanbul, Turkey. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data: (a) your phone number, used to create and secure your account, and your email address if you sign in with Google; (b) the profile details you provide — your name, date of birth (used to confirm you are 18 or older), gender, height, education, languages, bio, interests, prompt answers, and the optional lifestyle details you may add (what you do for work, what you are looking for, exercise, drinking, smoking and star sign); (c) your sexual orientation, where you choose to use Date mode, used only to show you and other people compatible profiles; (d) photos you upload to your profile; (e) the city you select in your profile, used to show approximate distances to other people — Teno does not read your device's location; (f) messages and interactions you exchange with other users, including likes, passes and matches — messages include the voice recordings you choose to send, which are stored so the person you sent them to can play them and are readable only by the two of you; (g) events you create or ask to join in Clubs, and the join requests you send or receive; (h) reports and blocks, including the reason text you provide when reporting; and (i) your visibility and account settings — whether you are in Date or Friends mode, whether you have paused your profile (Snooze), whether you limit who can see you in Date (Incognito), your Flash boost settings and remaining balance, and whether you have turned on the Loyalty Lock in a conversation, which the other person in that conversation can see and which hides your other chats once you have both turned it on; (j) the verification selfie you take, if you choose to verify your profile — see "Identity Verification" below; and (k) crash reports and diagnostic data, collected through Firebase Crashlytics when the app stops unexpectedly — the error and its stack trace, the app version, and basic device information such as the device model and operating system version. Crash data is used only to find and fix faults, is never used to advertise to you, and is switched off in development builds. Teno does not use an analytics SDK, an advertising SDK, or your device advertising identifier. We also collect basic technical and usage data necessary to operate the service.

Identity Verification

To confirm that a profile belongs to the person in its photos, Teno asks you to take a selfie. That selfie is compared with the photos on your profile. This comparison produces biometric data, which is a special category of personal data under KVKK (Article 6) and the GDPR (Article 9), and we process it only with your explicit consent, which you give by taking the selfie. Your verification selfie is never shown to other users and is never used for advertising. It is stored separately from your profile photos and only you and the verification process can access it. We keep it only as long as needed to verify your profile and to handle any dispute about that decision, and it is deleted when you delete your account. Where an external provider carries out the comparison, that provider acts as our data processor and may not use your image for any other purpose. If you do not want your face processed this way, do not submit a selfie; you can ask us to delete a selfie you already submitted at any time.

Sensitive Data

Two kinds of special-category data can arise in Teno: your sexual orientation, and the biometric data produced when a verification selfie is compared with your profile photos (see "Identity Verification"). Both are processed only with your explicit consent and only for the purpose described here. Your sexual orientation is treated as a special category of personal data under KVKK and the GDPR. We collect it only if you use Date mode, we use it only to decide which profiles you and others are shown, we never share it with third parties, and it is deleted with the rest of your data when you delete your account. You can stop providing it by switching to Friend mode, where matching is based on shared interests rather than gender or orientation.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to: create and secure your account; operate core features such as matching, distance display, and messaging; keep the community safe through reporting and blocking; respond to your requests; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where GDPR applies, we process your data on the following bases: performance of our contract with you (to provide the service); your consent (for example, for the photos you upload); our legitimate interests (such as safety and preventing abuse); and compliance with legal obligations. Under KVKK, we process personal data based on your explicit consent and the other legal grounds permitted by Article 5 of the law.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored and processed on Google Firebase servers located in Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3), within the European Union. Because Teno is operated from Turkey, your data may be transferred between Turkey and the EU. We rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers as required by KVKK and GDPR. The EU is recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection.

Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain records — such as reports and blocks — for safety, dispute resolution, or to meet legal obligations. Retention periods are limited to what is necessary for these purposes. If you begin creating your profile but do not finish, the answers you have given so far are saved on our servers so that you can carry on from another device. That unfinished draft is visible only to you, is deleted as soon as you complete sign-up, and is also deleted if you delete your account.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time from within the app or by contacting us at privacy@teno.club. Once verified, we will delete your data as described in the Data Retention section. Some anonymised or safety-related records may be kept where permitted or required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data; to object to certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to data portability. Under KVKK (Article 11), you also have the right to learn whether your data is processed and to request that unlawfully processed data be deleted. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@teno.club.

Consent

Where we rely on your consent — for example, to use the photos you add to your profile — you provide it when you enable the relevant feature, and you may withdraw it at any time through your device settings or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent may limit certain features of Teno. By using Teno, you consent to the processing described in this Policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, contact us at privacy@teno.club. Teno is operated from Istanbul, Turkey. This Policy may be updated from time to time; we will update the "last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
